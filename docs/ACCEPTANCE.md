# Targeted acceptance evidence

Checked 20 October 2026 against the deployed AWS runtime. This report distinguishes real transport, simulated factory information and remaining external blockers. It is not a claim that every failure mode or model response has been evaluated.

## Exercised conversation

A simulated material-readiness source request required stores, quality, planning and procurement information. The quality participant received a real SES email and replied through Gmail that a supplier certificate was needed. The received text remained immutable evidence. A bounded Bedrock agent proposed a supplier prerequisite; semantic review approved the grounded proposal. The dynamic case gained a supplier child while the quality task remained dependent on it.

The supplier response and its separate verification were explicitly simulated. Once verified, the coordinator sent a fresh quality request through real SES. A subsequent real Gmail response described 1,100 metre passing inspection, 100 metre quarantined and no stock release. After review and a distinct simulated verifier, the case completed with five verified requirements. The communication-only result was published through AWS SNS. A simulated requesting source separately acknowledged that exact result through the authenticated source API. No external factory system consumed or executed a command.

Stores, planning and procurement facts and verifier actions were also labelled synthetic. In particular, stores was answered through simulation while WhatsApp outbound was blocked; a successful WhatsApp conversation is not claimed.

## Outstanding external channel condition

Incoming-message evidence and failed outbound evidence are retained separately. Full real WhatsApp round-trip acceptance requires the account restriction to be cleared, then a new consented send/reply verification. Business verification status alone does not establish whether that restriction is lifted.
